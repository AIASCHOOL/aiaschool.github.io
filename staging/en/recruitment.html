Join us as a Japanese-language instructor

Aichi International Academy is recruiting full-time and part-time instructors. Please review the shared eligibility requirements and the terms for your preferred employment type before applying.

Eligibility requirements

Basic proficiency in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint

In addition, applicants must meet at least one of the following requirements:

  1. 1. A major or minor in Japanese-language education
  2. 2. Completion of a 420-hour Japanese-language teacher training course
  3. 3. Passing the Japanese Language Teaching Competency Test by March 31, 2024
  4. 4. Registered Japanese Language Teacher status
Positions and terms

FULL-TIME

Full-time Instructor

From ¥240,000/month

Benefits

  • Bonus twice a year
  • Full social insurance coverage
  • Transportation expenses provided

PART-TIME

Part-time Instructor

From ¥2,000 per 45-minute class

Benefits

  • Transportation expenses provided
How to apply

Please email your resume (with a photograph) and employment history.

so☆aiaso.gr.jp (Replace ☆ with @ before sending.)

Please include your mobile phone number and email address on your resume.

After document screening, we will conduct an interview and, if necessary, a mock lesson of about 20 minutes.
